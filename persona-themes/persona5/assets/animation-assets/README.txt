Animation assets
================

These files are loaded by Life Stats Log.html for the Persona 5 theme. Persona
3 and Persona 4 have their own copies under their theme folders. Keep the
filenames and formats below when replacing an asset.

progress-sparkle.svg
  The yellow burst shown when points increase. Replace with a transparent SVG
  using a 32 x 32 viewBox.

music-note.svg
  The notes that pop up beside the sparkle. Replace with a transparent SVG
  using a 32 x 32 viewBox.

points-increase.mp3
  The sound played when positive points are added. Replace with an MP3 audio
  file, keeping this filename.

button-click.wav
  The short sound played when a button is clicked. Replace with a WAV audio
  file, keeping this filename.

level-up.wav
  The chime played when a stat levels up. Replace with a WAV audio file,
  keeping this filename.

rank-up.svg
  The large RANK UP! banner used in the stats panel. Replace with an SVG image
  with a wide aspect ratio and transparent background.

8yj0h552lk681-sticker.png
  The floating sticker displayed in the stats panel and used as the site icon.
  Replace with a PNG, keeping this filename.

The sparkle and note effects are animated by CSS in Life Stats Log.css. Their
trigger conditions and image positions are configured in Life Stats Log.html.
