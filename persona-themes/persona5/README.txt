Persona 5 theme assets
======================

theme.css controls the Persona 5 palette and panel wallpaper.

assets/animation-assets contains the Persona 5 sticker, rank-up art, progress
effects, and sound files. Keep the filenames when replacing an asset.

assets/designs contains additional Persona 5 artwork and the Expose font.
assets/calendar contains the layered Persona 5 calendar artwork and its source
archive. The bundled calendar artwork includes its own LICENSE file.

The Persona 3 and Persona 4 folders contain independent starter copies of the
generic effects and sounds so they can be customized without changing Persona 5.
