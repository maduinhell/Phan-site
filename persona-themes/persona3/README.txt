Persona 3 theme assets
======================

theme.css controls the Persona 3 palette and panel background.

assets/animation-assets contains editable starter copies of the common sound
and progress-effect files. Replace these files independently from the
Persona 4 and Persona 5 folders, keeping their filenames.

The calendar artwork currently falls back to the bundled Persona 5 calendar.
Add Persona 3 artwork here when available and update the calendar asset path in
Life Stats Log.html to use it.
